# WP-11: `alerts` (alert list, summaries, toaster, tone, `AlertLayer`)

Branch `feat/WP-11-alerts`. Milestone U-M3 (the USSP's S-M2/S-M3
console and operator portal; the authority's A-M3 violations). Owns
`src/alerts/`, `src/layers/AlertLayer.tsx` exclusively. Depends on WP-7
(tracks, severity tokens), WP-8 (`AlertView` store, `FeedStatus`).
Consumers: the USSP console and portal, the authority console (with
`ViolationKind`), the ANSP coordination inbox.

## What to build

- `AlertSummary(alert, lang)`: one line per kind from `detail`, through
  the catalogue, with units and datums; `proximity`: "converging with
  {peer}: closest {d} m in {t} s, {v} m apart vertically" with the
  broadcast caveat when `peer.trust === "broadcast"`; `zone_incursion`:
  the zone id and type, plus "limit not judged ({reasons})" and "within
  widened band (pressure altitude)" when the flags say so; `height_
  exceedance`/`height_120m`: "{h} m over the limit"; `lost_link`: "no
  telemetry for {s} s" (not "lost"); `restriction_activated`: the
  restriction and whether the authorisation was updated or withdrawn;
  `nonconformance_nearby`: "a nearby aircraft deviated"; a cleared alert:
  "cleared: {reason} ({its numbers})". An unknown `detail` key is never
  invented: missing numbers are dashes.
- `AlertList`: sorted critical first then `raisedAt` descending; state
  badge; `clearReason` on held clears; `onAcknowledge` only when
  `canAcknowledge` (the app passes the role decision); `onSelect` to
  centre the map (the app's `flyTo`); keyboard operable; a live region
  announcing new critical alerts.
- `AlertToaster`: toasts for `raised` and for a severity rise (C-07);
  `useAlertTone` with Web Audio (a short synthesised tone, no file),
  repeating every `repeatMs` while an unacknowledged critical alert
  exists, starting only after a user gesture (a visible "enable sound"
  control until then, so a muted console is a visible state, E-02).
- `AlertLayer`: `proximity` as a line between the two aircraft positions
  from the track map, coloured by severity, dashed when either party is
  `broadcast`; `zone_incursion`, `height_*`, `nonconformance` as a ring
  on the aircraft; nothing for `cleared`; a party absent from the track
  map draws a ring on the known one and counts `alert_peer_missing`.

## Safety notes

This is the operator-facing end of the safety path. The kit must not
soften (a critical shown as a warning), must not stack (ten toasts for
one alert), must not overstate (C-12) and must not fall silent (a muted
tone is a visible state; a cleared alert says why). It computes
nothing: `t_cpa_s` is the server's number and the summary shows it with
its unit. The predecessor's resolution advice (P5-08, P5-09, P5-16) is
dropped by the spec (`07 §2`, C-11): no "descend" or "hold" text exists
here, and `CLAUDE.md` forbids adding one.
