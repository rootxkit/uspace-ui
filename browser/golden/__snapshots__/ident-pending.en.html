• registeredThe serial and the operator match an active registration. No mark beside the symbol —not provided
  • ! suspendedRegistered, but the aircraft or its operator is suspended or revoked. Mark beside the symbol: ! —not provided
  • ? unknown operatorThe serial is not registered, or the operator given is missing, unknown or not the owner. Mark beside the symbol: ? —not provided
  • × unidentifiedNo usable serial number: nobody can say what aircraft this is. Mark beside the symbol: × 1 track
  • … pendingNot judged yet: the provider's details have not been fetched, or the registry could not be reached. Neither unidentified nor an alarm. Mark beside the symbol: … 2 tracks
  • – no identificationNo identification has been received for this track. Mark beside the symbol: – —not provided
